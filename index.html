About us

What Chayeinu means

The name comes from the words said each night at the close of Maariv — כי הם חיינו ואורך ימינו, for they are our life and the length of our days. It is a claim about Torah: not an obligation carried alongside a life, but the thing the life is made of.

Congregation Chayeinu, Our Life Inc. was founded in Brooklyn in 2026 as a New York religious corporation. Our chartered purposes are simple and we have kept to them: to maintain a house of worship in the traditions of the Jewish faith, to conduct the communal affairs a viable community needs, and to provide for religious education according to the principles of that faith.

A place to daven

Nusach Chabad, twice a day, every day. Small enough that you will be noticed if you come and missed if you stop.

A place to learn

Torah study is not an add-on here. Our educational programming is the second half of what the congregation exists to do.

Leadership

Rabbi Shlomo Feldman

Rabbi Feldman founded Congregation Chayeinu after more than a decade of rabbinic work on three continents. He received semicha from Achei Temimim Chovevei Torah in 2022, served as a rabbi in Sydney, Australia, and continued his rabbinic service at the Aliya Institute before opening the congregation here in Crown Heights.

His approach in the shul is the same one that shapes the learning programs: meet a person where he actually is, not where a chart says he ought to be. He is reachable through the congregation for anything concerning the shul or its learning programs.

Education

Yeshivas Chayeinu

A program of the congregation

Providing for religious education is one of the congregation's chartered purposes, and Yeshivas Chayeinu is how we carry it out. It is a mesivta program for boys ages 14–17 — the ones for whom the mainstream yeshiva has stopped working but who do not belong in an intervention program either. There is very little for the boy in between. That is the boy we built this for.

What is learned

Chassidus with an eye toward practical application, applied Gemara, Sha'ar HaBitachon, Halacha, Chitas, and a daily Moshiach shiur. Learning skills are taught as skills, not assumed.

Enrollment

Private enrollment, tuition-based. Placement is by conversation with the Rabbi rather than by application form — we want to understand the boy before anyone commits.
